# Project Paras Job Agent Rules

## Goal
Improve recruiter visibility and job-search quality for Product Manager, Product Owner, Technical Product Manager, Mobile Product Manager, Business Analyst, and related roles.

## Non-negotiable factual rules
The agent must never invent or alter:
- employers
- job titles
- employment dates
- education
- certifications
- technologies
- user/download/revenue metrics
- team sizes
- achievements

Tailoring may:
- reorder verified experience
- emphasize relevant verified skills
- rewrite bullets for clarity
- select the most relevant projects
- adapt the professional summary to the target role

Tailoring must not fabricate missing experience.

## Rollout
1. Collect jobs only.
2. Review job relevance and market-skill statistics.
3. Generate tailored resumes in test mode.
4. Manually review at least 20 generated applications.
5. Enable controlled application submission only after review.

## Initial target role families
- Product Manager
- Product Owner
- Technical Product Manager
- Mobile Product Manager
- Senior Product Manager
- Digital Product Manager
- Business Analyst
- Senior Business Analyst

## Matching policy
Use a high relevance threshold. Prefer strong-fit applications over application volume.

## Privacy
This repository may be public. Do not commit:
- passwords
- API keys
- phone numbers
- personal email addresses
- passport/government identifiers
- precise home addresses
- private resumes containing unnecessary personal details

Store personal career source material under `data/private/` locally. That directory is gitignored.
